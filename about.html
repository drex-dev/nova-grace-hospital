About Nova Grace Hospital

A team of medical professionals serving Akbarpur and Kanpur Dehat since 2021.

Who we are

Big-city treatment, close to home

Nova Grace Hospital is a multi-speciality hospital at 979, Mati Road, Akbarpur. We opened so that families in Kanpur Dehat could get emergency care, surgery and specialist treatment at an affordable cost, without travelling to Kanpur or Lucknow.

Our 97 beds across 72 rooms include general wards, semi-private and private rooms, an ICU and a NICU. Specialists in medicine, surgery, gynaecology, orthopaedics, paediatrics, neurosurgery and chest medicine work alongside our own laboratory, radiology and pharmacy.
